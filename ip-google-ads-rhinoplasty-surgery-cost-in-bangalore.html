Best Rhinoplasty clinic in Bangalore | Nose jobs In Bangalore

Rhinoplasty is also known as a 'Nose job' or 'Nose Reshaping' surgery. A good nose is in right proportion and is in harmony with the face. Rhinoplasty is a plastic surgery which involves transforming the cartilages or the nasal bones in order to alter the shape of the nose.

Rhinoplasty can bring about size change, angle transformation, bridge straightening and tip reshaping of the nose. It can also correct certain breathing problems and mutilated or defaced nose as a consequence of birth defect or trauma. Overall, it can boost the features of the face and enhance one's appearance. It is usually a day care procedure where the skilled surgeon makes incisions underneath the nostrils and across the columella.

The cartilage and inner bone are reshaped to enhance the appearance of the nose. It must be noted that no nose is alike and every surgery is customised according to the anatomy of the nose and the expectations of the patient.

    What is the cost of rhinoplasty in Bangalore? I just want to understand the factors that decide the rhinoplasty surgery cost in Bangalore.

    The price of rhinoplasty or the cost of rhinoplasty in Bangalore depends on several factors. The doctor first analyzes your case and explains in detail about the treatment. Each treatment plan differs in terms of cost. Usually, the cost of rhinoplasty surgery in the city of Bangalore is between Rs. 50,000 and Rs. 1 lakh. If you can visit or seek rhinoplasty surgery in Midas Cosmetic Surgery Clinic, which is situated in 4th Block Jayanagar, Bangalore, the cost shall be affordable and optimal.

    I have decided to go for rhinoplasty surgery in Bangalore? May I know what the approximate success rate is?

    Well, rhinoplasty is usually considered to be the most challenging of all cosmetic surgeries or treatments. Even a minute modification in nasal contour necessitates a very vast knowledge of nasal composition and accurate surgical procedures. The success rate is higher if an experienced board-certified plastic surgeon operates.

    I got to know about rhinoplasty from one of my close friends. But, I am just afraid of the surgery. Does rhinoplasty affect my breathing?

    Many people have similar doubt. Yes. Rhinoplasty can affect breathing temporarily in the first week to 10 days when changes are performed in the septum and the bones. But, there is no negative effect on breathing in the long run.

    What can I drink and eat after a nose job?

    You are required to have soft foods for the first few days. It is also important to add a good quantity of fiber to your diet. Chewy foods need to be avoided. Moreover, alcohol and caffeine also need to be avoided, especially for the first few days following surgery. Drink a good amount of water before and after a nose job. One should avoid smoking for a few weeks after surgery.

    What is the difference between septoplasty and rhinoplasty?

    Yes. There exists a clear difference between a nose job, also known as rhinoplasty and septoplasty. It should be mentioned that the shape of the nose and breathing are interrelated and therefore, a nose job can sometimes be done not just to alter the shape of the nose but also to improve breathing via the nose. On the other hand, septoplasty is a surgery that is done to improve breathing through straightening the middle wall just inside the nose. Septoplasty may sometimes be combined with the procedure of rhinoplasty and is called Septorhinoplasty.

    Can I buy an insurance policy that reimburses the rhinoplasty cost in Bangalore or other cities?

    The answer to this question depends on two important elements. The first one is the insurance provider (insurance company) and the second one is the type of rhinoplasty. Insurance does not cover or reimburse the cost of rhinoplasty in Bangalore or other cities if you want to alter your nose for cosmetic reasons. But, the insurance policy may cover the cost of functional rhinoplasty, which is done to improve breathing by altering the inner passage(s) of the nose.

    Is a nose job a painful operation?

    You need to consult a doctor first and the doctor shall suggest the best treatment as per the requirements. Rhinoplasty whether done in Bangalore or other cities is usually not a painful operation since it is done under anesthesia. However, you might not feel mild pain after the surgery. The doctor will prescribe painkillers in the post-surgery recovery period. You don’t have to be afraid as the pain is usually mild and is usually for 3-5 days.

    I have enquired with two persons about the rhinoplasty surgery cost in Bangalore, but they told me different prices. Why does it differ?

    The cost of rhinoplasty cannot be the same for all. It differs from case to case and clinic to clinic. The operating doctor’s experience, training and skills, and facilities at place where the operation is performed also matters. Many people want to look better by changing the shape of the nose and some people may require the surgery for other medical reasons. The doctor will analyze your case and prescribe the right treatment. The cost of rhinoplasty surgery depends on the kind of treatment you require and other relevant factors.

    When should I seek rhinoplasty surgery?

    You may seek rhinoplasty surgery in Bangalore or wherever you want if you want to:

    • Minimize the width of your nostrils (base of nose)

    • Improve the shape of a nasal tip which is big

    • Correct a droopy nose. Flat nose, a long or a short nose)

    • Smoothen depressions or any bumps from the profile of your nose

    • Alter the shape of your nose and enhance the look by changing the proportion of your nose

    • Correct or modify general asymmetry and minimize your nasal bridge

    • Improve breathing if required

    • Modify or alter the appearance of the shape of the nasal bones
