RHINOPLASTY

Rhinoplasty Surgery In Bangalore (Nose Reshaping Surgery)

Rhinoplasty is also known as a 'Nose job' or 'Nose Reshaping' surgery. A good nose is in right proportion and is in harmony with the face. Rhinoplasty is a plastic surgery which involves transforming the cartilages or the nasal bones in order to alter the shape of the nose.

Rhinoplasty can bring about size change, angle transformation, bridge straightening and tip reshaping of the nose. It can also correct certain breathing problems and mutilated or defaced nose as a consequence of birth defect or trauma. Overall, it can boost the features of the face and enhance one's appearance. It is usually a day care procedure where the skilled surgeon makes incisions underneath the nostrils and across the columella.

The cartilage and inner bone are reshaped to enhance the appearance of the nose. It must be noted that no nose is alike and every surgery is customised according to the anatomy of the nose and the expectations of the patient.

What is a Nose Job?

Rhinoplasty or Nose Job is a surgery performed by an expert Cosmetic Plastic surgeon to alter or reshape the nose. It is usually performed to improve the features of the nose and make it appear in harmony with the face. Noses can be made to look smaller or bigger as per the requirement and suitability. Surgically, Rhinoplasty can also aid in improvement of nasal breathing. This procedure is called Functional Rhinoplasty.

What are the different types of Rhinoplasty?

1. Primary Rhinoplasty.

2. Secondary Rhinoplasty

What is Augmentation Rhinoplasty?

Augmentation Rhinoplasty is the procedure used to enhance the size of the nose and make it more proportionate. It is usually done to increase the projection of the dorsum and sometimes the tip of the nose.

What is Reduction Rhinoplasty?

Reduction Rhinoplasty is the procedure performed to reduce the size of the nose. This is usually required in patients with a dorsal hump, broad nasal bones, nasal tip which is big and a wide lower part of the nose.

Who is a good candidate for Rhinoplasty or Nose Job?

Any person who is healthy and desires to have a better shape of his or her nose, alter the appearance of the nose, rectify deformity and enhance the symmetry of the nose is an ideal candidate for Rhinoplasty surgery. Also, a detailed consultation with the surgeon will help the candidate to discuss his desired outcome as the surgeon analyses the anatomy of the nose in proportion to the face.

Common Indications for a Rhinoplasty are in someone:

1. whose nose is depressed or flat (Saddle nose).

2. with a humpnose.

3. with a broad tip nose (Bulbous tip).

4. with asymmetry or crooked nose.

5. Someone with the nasal tip which is either drooping or too upturned.

6. Someone whose nose is broad at the base or with wide nostrils.

7. To correct deformities in a person with previous injury to the nose.

8. To correct complications or deformities due to previous surgeries on the nose.

What are the different approaches to perform a Rhinoplasty?

'Open Rhinoplasty' is a surgical process where an incision is made on the columella (the skin between the two nostrils).

'Closed Rhinoplasty' or Endonasal rhinoplasty is a surgical process where the incisions are not visible and are concealed inside the nose.

The surgeon can use either technique depending on the anatomy of the nose,extent of the correction required and the desired result.

What anaesthesia is used for Rhinoplasty surgery?

Yes. Rhinoplasty surgery is usually done under general anaesthesia although minor corrections are possible under local anaesthesia.

Is there any minimum required age to undergo Rhinoplasty surgery?

Rhinoplasty Surgery can be done at any point of time once the nasal growth is complete. 16-17 years is the normal age when nasal growth is almost complete. If one requires reshaping of the bony portion of the nose by osteotomies, the procedure is delayed by 2-3 years to avoid any impact on the nasal growth. Nevertheless, it is important that the patient is mature emotionally and psychologically to understand clearly about what they wish to improvein their nose, the ability to understand the details of the surgery and its outcome and make an informed decision.

What is the cost involved for Rhinoplasty Surgery?

Rhinoplasty surgery cost varies depending on the complexity of the treatment, the experience, expertise, dexterity and skill of the surgeon, the setting etc. Depending on the alteration to be done, the surgeon arrives at an estimate. The cost could be less for just the tip refinement and more for a complete Rhinoplasty procedure. The period of anaesthesia, the requirement for cartilage grafts, medication,the complexity of the surgery, whether it is a primary or secondary surgery, hospitalisation fees and the surgeon's fees will be the factors that determine the cost of Rhinoplasty. How long does one have to stay in the hospital after Rhinoplasty surgery?

Most of the Rhinoplasty surgeries are done as day care surgeries and the patient is discharged on the same day.

What is the recovery period after a Rhinoplasty surgery?

One is advised rest and allowed only light activities in the first 7-10 days. Usually the stitches, tapesand the splint are removed one week after the surgery.The patient is socially presentable by 10-14 days and can return to work. In order to return to full physical activity, a period of 6 weeks to 3 months is required depending on the procedure performed. It is strongly advised to avoid physical exertion in the first few weeks after the surgery.

How do we select a perfect surgeon for Rhinoplasty Surgery?

Rhinoplasty surgery is usually performed by Cosmetic Plastic Surgeons. Look for a surgeon who has affiliations with recognised boards. A good plastic surgeon has certifications from recognised boards. Look for the former patient testimonials, the clinical experience, and technical competence. Also check out the operating centre for its infrastructure and facilities. A good surgeon will discuss about all the details of the surgery, the recovery and complications with the patient after understanding the patient's desired goals.

What is Non-Surgical Rhinoplasty?

Non-Surgical Rhinoplasty, also called as 'Liquid Rhinoplasty' or 'Liquid nose job' involves the use of injectable fillers to bring about the desired changes in the size and shape of the nose. The shape of the nasal dorsum and the tip can be altered to suit the patient's needs. The procedure is done on out-patient basis using a numbing cream and is painless. The changes are visible immediately but take about 7-10 days to settle down. The results generally last for 9-12 months.

What are the indications for Non-Surgical Rhinoplasty?

Non-Surgical Rhinoplasty is usually indicated in those who want an improvement in the shape of the nose without a surgery. It is usually used in treating minor cosmetic deficits of the nose.

It can be used to improve

1. Minor bumps and irregularities.

2. Minor degrees of asymmetry of nose

3. Tip corrections where a minor change is desired.

4. After a surgical rhinoplasty to correct minor defects.
